Quick start
  1. Choose an Excel or CSV workbook and select its worksheet.
  2. Confirm the detected columns and select Apply mapping.
  3. Choose the production day and shift settings.
  4. Set Jobs to plan, or enable Fill available shift.
  5. Select Run schedule.
  6. Review the schedule, adjust WO groups if needed, then print or export it.

Important: Apply mapping confirms the spreadsheet layout. Run schedule creates or refreshes the schedule.

Workbook and column mapping
  • EZDES accepts .xlsx, .xls, and .csv files.
  • Copperweight and Speed are required. WO, TN, Number of cores, and Due date can also be mapped.
  • A job is identified by the first two WO segments. For example, 174-05-01 and 174-05-08 are one WO job: 174-05.
  • Jobs to plan counts WO groups while keeping every spreadsheet line belonging to each selected job.
  • The latest-file folder option requires the EZDES server; ordinary file upload works in the standalone HTML.
Rule presets and timing
  • A preset saves core-weight rules, production day, shift settings, setup minutes, and column mapping in this browser.
  • Target speed, first-core travel time, and next-core interval can be adjusted under Core-weight rules.
  • When the next line uses the same effective weight, EZDES skips another first-core travel delay.
  • Mixed weights use the heaviest component for speed and timing.
  • Loading a preset changes settings; select Run schedule to rebuild the result.
Priority, capacity, and summary
  • Monday favors lighter copperweights, Wednesday is balanced, and Friday favors heavier weights.
  • Fill available shift selects prioritized WO jobs until the next job would exceed the available time.
  • Weight changes counts transitions between effective weights in the arranged sequence.
  • Weight mix counts scheduled WO jobs by effective weight.
  • Internal priority and placement explanations are planning aids and are not printed.
Search, pinning, and rearranging WO jobs
  • Find TN or WO group filters the on-screen arranged data. Matching one line keeps its complete WO job visible.
  • Pin keeps a WO group at the top when the schedule is rebuilt.
  • In Schedule positions, select one numbered position and then another to swap the complete WO jobs.
  • Drag a row in Arranged data. A green line shows the exact insertion point and the entire WO group moves together.
  • Focus a schedule row or numbered position and use Up Arrow or Down Arrow to move the complete job.
  • Undo, Redo, and Reset manual order manage schedule adjustments.
Mixed weight layers
  • The panel follows the current arranged schedule and lists only its mixed-weight layers.
  • Clear means the bottom is copper-only and remains eligible for normal grouping.
  • Bottom image keeps that layer out of normal similarity grouping and applies its targeted changeover when appropriate.
Notes, printing, and Excel export
  • Select the note icon under internal priority to add instructions for a WO job. A dot indicates a saved note.
  • The full note stays out of the on-screen table and prints as a wrapped row beneath its job.
  • The compact landscape print omits target speed, scheduled hours, start/finish, placement reasoning, and internal priority.
  • Printing always includes every scheduled job even when an on-screen filter is active.
  • Excel export includes the arranged data, calculated planning fields, and job notes.
Troubleshooting
  • If Run schedule produces no result, confirm Copperweight and Speed mapping and review Data checks.
  • Make sure every copper weight in the workbook has a core-weight rule.
  • If the standalone HTML cannot read Excel, reconnect to the internet and refresh so its workbook-reading library can load.
  • Presets, notes, pins, mixed-layer choices, and manual order are stored only in the current browser.
